## Nitesh Data — Co-Founder

An engineering graduate with an entrepreneurial mindset, Nitesh Data joined the family business after completing his B.Tech. His ambition to build and experiment led him towards developing the Group’s foodservice disposables and packaging segment.

He combines an engineer’s approach to problem-solving with an entrepreneur’s instinct for opportunity, listening to the market and understanding customer requirements to develop practical business solutions.

Closely involved in product offerings and the direction of this growing segment, he sees entrepreneurship as a continuous pursuit of curiosity, responsiveness and better ways to create value.

Editorial approach summary: Understanding the market. Identifying opportunities. Creating practical solutions.
